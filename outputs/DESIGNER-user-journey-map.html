Current State (Without ClinicalFlow)
Login to AnyCompanyEMR. Separate AnyCompany Insight AI portal login. Two different auth systems.
Search patient in EMR. Open chart. Manually copy relevant history. Switch to AnyCompany Insight AI system.
No real-time guidelines. Must manually search clinical databases. AnyCompany Insight AI gives opaque scores with no reasoning.
No explainability. "Black box" confidence score. Cannot verify AI logic. Must trust blindly or ignore.
Switch to CPOE system. Re-enter patient details. Manually transcribe orders. Risk of errors.
14+ min per consultation. Multiple systems open. Cognitive fatigue. Documentation scattered.
Friction Points
Multiple credentials. Password fatigue. Session timeouts across systems.
Context switching. Data re-entry. Incomplete patient picture across systems.
Outdated guidelines. No confidence scoring. AI suggestions arrive too late to influence decisions.
Low AI trust. Physicians bypass AI entirely. No audit trail for clinical reasoning.
Transcription errors. Wrong dosages. Delayed orders. Manual reconciliation needed.
Burnout. Rushed decisions. Missed diagnoses (est. 15-20% preventable). Physician churn risk.
Desired State (With ClinicalFlow™)
Single SSO login. One workspace. Immediate access to all tools. 3 seconds to productive state.
Patient context auto-loads. Full history, labs, meds visible in left panel. Zero manual lookup.
Real-time AI guidelines appear as patient loads. Confidence scores (HIGH/MED/LOW). Evidence-cited recommendations.
Chain-of-thought reasoning visible. "Why this recommendation" expandable. Guideline citations linked. Full transparency.
One-click "Accept & Order." Auto-populated dosage/route. Safety checks inline. Override with reason capture.
8 min avg consultation (40% faster). Single system. Confident decisions. Complete audit trail. Reduced burnout.
